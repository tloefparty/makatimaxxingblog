About the Author ✨

Hello! I'm Ysa a student, aspiring IT professional, and a self-proclaimed Salcedo Girly. 🌸

When I'm not drowning in schoolwork or helping organize SOCIT events, you can usually find me romanticizing my life somewhere in the heart of Makati. Whether that means hunting down the best matcha in Legazpi Village, getting budoled at a weekend pop-up in Glorietta, or just sitting under the trees at Washington SyCip Park with my VECO journal, I am fully committed to the art of Makati Maxxing.

  • 📖 Currently Consuming: Fantasy Webtoons (Navier from The Remarried Empress) and indie music like Malcolm Todd and Clairo.
  • 🎨 Hobbies: Cafe hopping, solo art dates, and Journaling my thoughts.

I created this digital diary as a cozy corner to document my favorite city spots, review my current hyperfixations, and share my journey through college. Grab a drink, play some good music, and stay a while!
