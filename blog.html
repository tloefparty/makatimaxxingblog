1. Makati Mall spots in San Antonio

▼

I've grown up going to Glorietta and the malls beside it. I know the mall like the back of my hand, and I have a list of my favorite spots to eat, study, and just hang out. In this entry, I share my top 5 spots in Glorietta that are perfect for a solo date or a chill afternoon with friends.

1. Manmaru glorietta - yummy filo food and recently renovated. Very nice ambiance and my favorite resto to eat at!
2. SM Supermarket turon - BUKO TURON THE GOAT, tastes like eggpie but wrapped in turon!!
3. Chuck's steak & chops landmark - the best java rice, gravy, and burger steak for steal price! Amoy pa lang busog ka na!
4. Nanyang greenbelt - the best laksa, creamy and spicy, perfect for rainy days. The ambiance is also very cozy and chill.
5. Cha tuk chak glorietta - the best thai milk tea, not too sweet and great for a pick me up! would not reco their dine in tho

5. Lowkey GEMS in makati

▼

Have you ever been to a place that feels whimsy? One of the best places there is in Makati is the Makati Cinema Square, lots of fun activities such as the gun range, airsoft, crystal shops, ukay, and more. Moreover, it is a great place to try out the Aida's michelen star restaurant, the ambiance is very cozy and the food is amazing! Another lowkey gem is the Salcedo Saturday Market, lots of good food and fresh produce. Though, the prices are quite high, but the quality is worth it.

September 10, 2026

9. Artsy Solo Dates

▼

A lot of good art museums are in Makati, specifically Paseo De Magallanes, a whole straight street filled with Art exhibitions. I love going to these places and just admiring the art. I also like to take photos and draw inspiration from the art. I recommend going to these places alone, as it allows you to fully immerse yourself in the art and appreciate it more. I also recommend bringing a sketchbook and drawing some of the art you see, it is a great way to practice your skills and learn from the masters.
